# Delivery schedule — hard deadline Saturday 2026-09-12

Usage capacity is elevated until Sunday and drops sharply after. Everything
below is scoped to fit that window. **Scope cuts are decisions, not
compromises to apologise for — each is stated in the paper as a limitation.**

## Scope cuts (accepted, record as ADR)

| Cut | Was | Now | Justification for the paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Datasets | PubMedQA + MedQA + MedMCQA | **MedQA only** | Focused study on the primary novelty benchmark; breadth and binary-parity claims deferred as stated future work |
| Grid | 8 variants × 3 datasets × 3 seeds | **V1, V3, V5, V4−audit, V4−conflict on MedQA, single seed, n=500** | The five cells that test the actual contributions; V0/V2/V4 covered by the Phase 3 B-baselines |
| RAGAS faithfulness | Required metric | **Dropped** | NLI entailment against cited evidence covers factual fidelity; RAGAS adds a heavy dependency and a second judge model for correlated signal |
| Phase 6 harness | Resumable, parallel, benchmarked | **Minimal: run, score, write** | Concurrency benchmarking is an engineering nicety, not a result |
| C4 "parity on PubMedQA" | A contribution | **Removed from §3** | No code path exists (`run_baselines.py` accepts medqa/medmcqa only); do not claim it |

**Kept because they are cheap and high-value:** the Gemini Flash sensitivity
check (200 questions, free tier) — it defends the B3 verifier-collapse result,
which is the paper's strongest finding.

## Non-negotiables even under deadline

- No number in the paper that isn't traceable to `results/`
- No "A beats B" without a paired-bootstrap CI
- Every cut above stated explicitly as a limitation
- Negative results reported as findings (ADR-012, ADR-018, ADR-025 precedent)

Deadline pressure is exactly when these get quietly dropped. They are what
makes this defensible.

## The realistic risk

Phase 7's grid is ~33 GPU-hours for the four agent variants at n=500. It
**must** launch Wednesday night. If Phase 5 or 6 slips past Wednesday, cut the
grid to n=300 rather than cutting cells — fewer questions weakens the CIs, but
dropping a cell removes an answer to "does this component earn its place",
which is the whole point of the ablation.
